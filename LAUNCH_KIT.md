## 1. Target Audience & Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary Creator Segments
1. **Video Podcasters & Show Hosts (45% of outreach)**:
   - *Profile*: Weekly 45–90 min episodes on YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Podcasts with 15k–250k subscribers/listeners.
   - *Core Pain Point*: Heavy post-production overhead and zero vertical video distribution on Shorts/Reels/TikTok.
   - *Hook*: Delivering a done-for-you, pre-rendered 45-second vertical clip with kinetic captions and balanced audio stems from their latest episode.
2. **YouTubers & Video-First Builders (30% of outreach)**:
   - *Profile*: Long-form educational, tech, or business video creators (10k–500k subscribers).
   - *Core Pain Point*: Difficulty repurposing horizontal 16:9 video into multi-platform text carousels and vertical video without hiring dedicated agency staff.
   - *Hook*: Automated 3-minute turnaround converting a 20-minute video into 3 high-hook Shorts and a LinkedIn PDF carousel.
3. **Expert-Led Creators (Coaches, Consultants, Founders) (25% of outreach)**:
   - *Profile*: Solopreneurs and B2B advisors with courses ($497–$1,500), paid communities, or consulting retainers ($2k–$10k/mo).
   - *Core Pain Point*: High client delivery workload leaving zero time for daily multi-platform content repurposing.
   - *Hook*: Turning 1 weekly client webinar or coaching call into 10 publish-ready LinkedIn carousels and X threads.

### Geographic Scope
- **Approved Developed English-Speaking Markets**: United States (53.1%), United Kingdom (17.1%), Canada (12.6%), Australia (9.5%), Singapore (3.8%), New Zealand (2.1%), Ireland (1.8%).
- **Compliance Rules**: 100% English recent content, public consented business contact addresses, and explicit opt-out mechanisms.

## 3. Thirty-Day Step-by-Step Implementation Plan

### Week 1: Infrastructure Initialization & Priority A Pilot (Days 1–7)
- **Monday (Day 1)**:
  - Register 2 secondary sending domains: `tryincubrix.com` and `getincubrix.com`.
  - Configure DNS records: SPF (`v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all`), DKIM (2048-bit Google Workspace key), DMARC (`v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@incubrix.com`).
  - Connect secondary domains to Instantly.ai and initiate automated warmup (warmup ramp: 5 emails/day, target: 40/day by Day 14).
- **Tuesday (Day 2)**:
  - Configure LinkedIn outreach profiles with optimized banners, headline ("Growth Lead @ IncuBrix | Helping 500+ Creators 10x Repurposed Content"), and featured demo link.

  - Dispatch Touch 1 to the initial cohort of 25 Priority A leads across Email and LinkedIn.

  - Log initial responses; respond within 15 minutes to any interested prospect.
- **Friday (Day 5)**:
  - Review Week 1 metrics: Open rate target > 65%, 0 spam complaints, first 2 demo calls booked.

  - Conduct A/B testing on email subject lines:

- **End-of-Week Milestone (Day 14)**:

  - Automated dispatch of Touch 3 (Graceful Breakup + Asset Vault link) to non-responders from Week 1.

## 4. Safe Daily Volume Ramp & Deliverability Guardrails

| Outreach Channel | Week 1 Daily Cap | Week 2 Daily Cap | Week 3 Daily Cap | Week 4 Daily Cap | Monthly Total | Platform Safety Ceiling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Email (Domain A)** | 10 emails/day | 20 emails/day | 35 emails/day | 35 emails/day | ~650 emails | Max 40/day/inbox |
| **Email (Domain B)** | 10 emails/day | 20 emails/day | 35 emails/day | 35 emails/day | ~650 emails | Max 40/day/inbox |
| **LinkedIn (Seat 1)** | 10 msgs/day | 15 msgs/day | 20 msgs/day | 25 msgs/day | ~420 msgs | Max 30/day/account |
| **X / Twitter DMs** | 5 DMs/day | 10 DMs/day | 15 DMs/day | 15 DMs/day | ~275 DMs | Max 25/day/account |
| **Total Daily Touches** | **35 / day** | **65 / day** | **105 / day** | **110 / day** | **~2,000 touches** | Safe across all platforms |

### Deliverability Guardrails
1. **Never Send from Primary Domain**: All outreach originates exclusively from secondary domains (`tryincubrix.com`, `getincubrix.com`) forwarding to `incubrix.com`.
2. **Strict Text-Only First Touch**: Zero attachments, zero images, and zero tracking pixels in Touch 1. Only clean plain text with an opt-out footer.
3. **Smart In-Flight Throttling**: If bounce rate exceeds 2% or spam report rate exceeds 0.08%, the engine automatically pauses dispatches for 24 hours.
